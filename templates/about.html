Who We Are

Built around
the traveler.

FRIDAY RETREATS is the travel brand of F-Day Travel Services Pvt. Ltd., established in 2024 and based in Peermade, Kerala.

Our goal is simple: make travel easier from the moment you start planning until you return home.

Instead of making travelers coordinate with multiple services for destinations, packages, accommodation, documentation, payments, and other travel requirements, Friday Retreats brings these experiences together through one platform.

We combine technology with personalized travel support to create a travel experience that is simple, transparent, convenient, and memorable.
